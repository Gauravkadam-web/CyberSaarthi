## 1. Verified Official Indian Cybercrime Reporting Redirections

In accordance with Section 2 and Section 13 of `AGENTS.md` and `PRD.md`, CyberSaarthi is strictly an educational support prototype and **NOT** an official law enforcement agency. The portal must prominently feature and redirect users to verified, authentic Indian government cybercrime helplines and portals.

| Authority / Initiative | Purpose | Official Portal URL | Helpline / Contact | Notes for UI Display |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP)** | Official Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA / I4C) citizen portal for lodging formal cybercrime complaints online | [https://cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) | **1930** (24x7 Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Helpline) | Prominently display on top of every page and in the incident report submission flow. |
| **Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting & Management System (CFCFRMS)** | Immediate financial fraud freeze (banking & UPI) | [https://cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) | **1930** (Golden hour reporting) | Display urgent notice for UPI, debit card, credit card, net-banking losses. |
| **Sanchar Saathi / Chakshu (DoT)** | Reporting suspected fraudulent communication (SMS, calls, WhatsApp) and mobile block/lost requests | [https://sancharsaathi.gov.in](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in) | N/A | Provide link in "Phishing and Social Engineering" and "Job Scams" awareness topics. |
| **RBI Complaint Management System (CMS)** | Banking ombudsman complaints when financial institution fails to resolve fraud | [https://cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in) | **14448** (RBI Financial Education) | Provide link under banking safety resources. |
| **CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)** | National nodal agency for responding to computer security incidents | [https://www.cert-in.org.in](https://www.cert-in.org.in) | `incident@cert-in.org.in` | Reference for malware, ransomware, and high-impact security incidents. |

## 2. Standardized Disclaimer Copy for UI Components

To maintain legal clarity and avoid misleading victims, the following exact text strings must be used across UI components.

### 2.1 Persistent Global Header Banner
```text
⚠️ Academic Project Notice: CyberSaarthi is an academic cyber-awareness prototype, NOT an official government or police complaint portal. In case of financial fraud, call 1930 immediately or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
```

### 2.2 Pre-Submission Incident Report Modal / Warning
```text
Important Notice Before You Proceed:
Submitting an incident report on CyberSaarthi documents your experience for awareness and academic analysis only. 
This DOES NOT file an official police complaint (FIR) and DOES NOT initiate a police investigation or fund recovery.

• If you have suffered an immediate financial loss, dial 1930 immediately to freeze fraudulent transactions during the golden hour.
• File your official legal complaint at https://cybercrime.gov.in.
• DO NOT submit sensitive passwords, OTPs, PINs, full debit/credit card numbers, or government ID scans in this form.
```

### 2.3 Advisory ML Prediction Disclaimer
```text
ℹ️ Advisory Category Suggestion:
The suggested category below is generated by a machine-learning text classification model for guidance purposes only. It does not constitute a legal determination, formal accusation, or verified fact. An administrator will review your report.
```

### 2.4 Status Tracking Screen Disclaimer
```text
Portal Tracking Note:
The statuses displayed below (e.g., SUBMITTED, UNDER REVIEW, REVIEWED) represent internal CyberSaarthi portal workflows only. They do not reflect law enforcement, police, or judicial progress.
```

### 2.5 Report Export / Print Summary Watermark
```text
"ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE REPORT — FOR AWARENESS AND PERSONAL RECORDING ONLY — NOT AN OFFICIAL POLICE DOCUMENT"
```

## 3. UI Display Rules & Constraints

1. **Persistent Visibility:** The top alert banner must be visible on both mobile and desktop viewports across all public and authenticated pages.
2. **Explicit User Acknowledgment:** The report submission form must require a mandatory checkbox:
   - `[x] I understand that CyberSaarthi is an academic educational portal, not an official police complaint filing system, and that submitting this report does not register an FIR.`
3. **No False Promises:** Never use wording such as *"We are investigating your fraud"*, *"Our team will recover your money"*, or *"Offender has been flagged for arrest"*.
4. **Verified Helpline Highlighting:** The number **1930** must always be styled as a clickable `tel:1930` link for mobile users.
